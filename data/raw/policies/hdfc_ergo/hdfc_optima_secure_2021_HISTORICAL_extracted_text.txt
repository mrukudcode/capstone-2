[PAGES 15-16] SECTION D. GENERAL TERMS.
1.3 Claim Settlement (Penal Interest) 2021 version: Company shall settle/reject claim within 30 days from receipt
of LAST NECESSARY DOCUMENT; if investigation warranted, complete within 30 days and settle/reject within 45 days
of last necessary document; delay beyond 45 days -> interest at 2% above Bank Rate.
[THIS DIFFERS SUBSTANTIALLY FROM THE 2026 VERSION, WHICH INSTEAD COMMITS TO SETTLING WITHIN 15 DAYS FROM DATE OF
RECEIPT OF INTIMATION OF CLAIM (not "last necessary document"), WITH NO SEPARATE 45-DAY INVESTIGATION-CASE
CARVE-OUT STATED IN THE EXTRACTED 2026 TEXT.]
1.6 Moratorium Period (2021 version): "After completion of EIGHT continuous years under the policy, no look
back to be applied." [THIS DIFFERS FROM THE 2026 VERSION, WHICH SETS MORATORIUM AT SIXTY CONTINUOUS MONTHS
(5 years), matching the post-2020 IRDAI regulatory standard. The 2021 document's 8-year moratorium reflects the
pre-2020 regulatory regime and is a genuine, material, version-specific difference -- not an extraction error.]
1.8 Free look Period (2021 version): "fifteen days from date of receipt of the policy document" [DIFFERS FROM
2026 VERSION'S 30 DAYS -- IRDAI increased the standard free-look period to 30 days for policies with risk
commencement on/after 1 Oct 2024 per the Insurance Products Regulations 2024 / Master Circular 29-May-2024;
this 2021 document pre-dates that change].
1.9 Renewal of Policy (2021): Company "not under obligation to give any notice for Renewal" [2026 version places
more affirmative renewal-continuity language and IRDAI-regulation-driven provisions, e.g. "no fresh
underwriting unless SI increase" is stated explicitly in 2026 text but not found in this 2021 extract].
1.10 Portability (2021): apply "at least 45 days before, but not earlier than 60 days from the policy renewal
date" [DIFFERS FROM 2026 VERSION'S 30-DAYS-BEFORE FIGURE -- IRDAI's portability lead time was subsequently
shortened from 45 to 30 days].
1.12 Cancellation (2021): Policyholder gives 15 days' written notice, refund per a SHORT-RATE CANCELLATION GRID
by elapsed policy month and tenure (values reproduced above); Company may cancel on fraud/misrepresentation/
non-disclosure with 15 days' written notice, no refund. [DIFFERS FROM 2026 VERSION: Policyholder notice period
is 7 days in 2026 vs 15 days in 2021; 2026 grants pro-rata refund on Policyholder-initiated cancellation rather
than the 2021 short-rate grid.]
2.1 Non-Disclosure/Misrepresentation of PED (2021): additional waiting period option "not exceeding 4 years"
[DIFFERS FROM 2026 VERSION'S "not exceeding 3 years"].
2.4 Loadings (2021): maximum medical underwriting loading "shall not exceed 100% for each condition and a total
of 150% for each Insured Person," proposer must respond to counter-offer letter "within 15 days." [This detailed
loading-cap clause was not found in the 2026 extracted sections captured in this session; flagged
NOT_FOUND_IN_SOURCE for 2026 pending fuller extraction of 2026 Section D.2, not a confirmed absence.]
